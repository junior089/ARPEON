# Code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully regardless of background, identity, musical ability or
technical experience. Give specific, constructive feedback about work. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory language, doxxing and unwanted personal contact are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful contributions or restrict participation, proportionate
to severity and repetition. Explain decisions where doing so does not expose private
information. Handle reports privately and avoid retaliation.

A private moderation contact must be established before inviting public participation.
Do not publish another person's identifying information in a report.

This project-specific policy is licensed with the repository; it is not a copied covenant.
