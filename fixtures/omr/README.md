# Original OMR fixtures

`first-notes.png` is the original melody in `../musicxml/first-notes.musicxml`,
rendered by the application's OpenSheetMusicDisplay 2.2.0 and Chromium renderer
at three times the SVG dimensions (1758 × 2487 pixels). `first-notes.pdf` wraps
those pixels losslessly on a single A4 page. Both are AGPL-3.0-or-later original
test material. No published score or recording was used.

They contain C4 and D♯4 quarter notes, a half rest, and an A4 whole note, in two
4/4 measures. OMR accuracy must be measured against this source; successfully
exporting XML alone does not establish correct recognition. These deliberately
small fixtures do not establish performance on complex or degraded scores.
